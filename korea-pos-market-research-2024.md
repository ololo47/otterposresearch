## 6. Regulatory & Compliance Requirements

### E-Tax Invoice System (Critical Requirement)

**Mandatory for VAT-Registered Businesses:**
- Businesses with **annual revenue >₩100 million** must issue electronic invoices (as of July 2023)
- All invoices transmitted to **National Tax Service (NTS)** within **1 day** of issuance
- Digital signature required
- **5-year record retention** mandatory

**Format Requirements:**
- XML format following e-Tax Invoice standard
- Real-time compliance verification
- Integration with ERP/POS systems

**Important: Software-Based Compliance**
- Korea does **NOT** require specialized fiscal hardware (unlike some countries)
- Standard POS can be used if software integrates with NTS e-Tax portal
- Cloud POS systems handle this automatically

### Financial Reporting

**VAT Requirements:**
- **10% VAT** collected and remitted quarterly
- Filing deadline: **25 days after quarter end**
- VAT invoices required for all transactions

**Accounting Standards:**
- Listed companies: K-IFRS mandatory
- Unlisted: K-IFRS or K-GAAP optional
- Financial statements + business report filed annually

**Record Keeping:**
- Tax invoices, credit notes, import docs: **5 years minimum**
- POS must generate compliant documentation
