## 1. Coffee Shops & Cafes

### Official Government Statistics

**100,729 coffee shops** at end of 2022 (Statistics Korea / 통계청)
- **+4,292** from previous year
- Surpassed 100,000 for first time in 2022
- More than doubled from 2016 levels

**Revenue & Employment (2022):**
- Combined sales: **15.5 trillion won** ($11.2 billion)
- Employees: **~270,000 workers** nationwide
- **84,000 cafes** (84%) had fewer than 4 employees

**Source:** [Korea Herald - Cafe Count Surpasses 100,000](https://m.koreaherald.com/article/3426034); [Korea Times - Coffee Nation](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/www/tech/2024/12/419_377739.html) (citing Statistics Korea official data)

### Franchise Data (2023)

**886 coffee chain brands** in Korea
- Fair Trade Commission official count

**Top Franchises by Store Count (2023):**
- Ediya Coffee: **3,000+ stores**
- Mega Coffee: **2,156 stores**
- **~25,000 total franchise coffee shops**

**Operating Hours:**
- **81.4%** of coffee/beverage shops have **no regular holidays**
- Most open 10-12 hours/day

**Source:** [Statista citing Fair Trade Commission](https://www.statista.com/statistics/829232/south-korea-cafe-chain-store-number-by-franchise-brand/); [Korea Herald - Coffee Shop Operations](https://m.koreaherald.com/article/10472076)

### Cafe Segment Growth Projections

**Specialist coffee/tea shops:**
- Euromonitor projects **5.2% CAGR** to 2029
- Reaching **$471.4 million by 2029**

**Cafes & bars segment:**
- Projected **15.58% CAGR** by value (2024-2029)

**Source:** [Euromonitor - Consumer Foodservice South Korea](https://www.euromonitor.com/consumer-foodservice-in-south-korea/report)

**Note on Euromonitor:** Reputable market research firm with transparent methodology, but may be considered commercial source. Use at your discretion.

## 2. Full-Service Restaurants (FSR)

### Market Dominance

**63% of South Korea foodservice market** in 2024
- Largest segment
- Full table service, waitstaff, sit-down dining

**Independent outlets:** 73% market share
- Most FSRs are independently owned vs franchised

**Source:** Multiple market analysis reports citing industry data (Euromonitor, industry associations)

### Traditional Korean Restaurants (Hansik)

**504,657 Korean food businesses** (end of December 2024)
- **-1.6% decline** from 512,979 in 2023
- Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs data

**Market Share Decline:**
- **41.8% of all eateries** in 2024 (down from 45.6% in 2018)
- **6 consecutive years** of declining share
- Traditional independent restaurants struggling

**BUT - Korean Franchise Restaurants Growing:**
- Korean franchise brands: **3,701** (+4.1% in 2024)
- Korean franchise locations: **41,353** (+3.7% growth)
- "Korean food is now one of the top 3 sectors along with fried chicken and coffee" - Korea Franchise Association

**Source:** [NewKerala citing Ministry of Agriculture](https://www.newkerala.com/news/o/number-firms-korean-food-business-drops-2024-data-220); [Korea Herald - Traditional Korean Eateries](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10536213) (Fair Trade Commission data)

## 3. Quick Service Restaurants (QSR) / Fast Food

### Fried Chicken Market (Major QSR Category)

**41,436 total fried chicken eateries** in 2022
- **71% are franchises** (29,358 franchise outlets)
- Up from 64% franchised in 2020
- Chicken is one of Korea's top 3 franchise sectors

**Market consolidation:**
- Total chicken eateries decreased from 41,742 (2020) to 41,436 (2022)
- But franchise share increased

**Source:** [Korea Herald - Franchise Chicken Dominance](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/3462282) (Fair Trade Commission data, August 2024)

### QSR Technology Trends

**Automation & Digital Ordering:**
- Automated kiosks and robot chefs in Seoul QSR locations
- AI-based ordering systems for speed/accuracy
- Mobile app ordering widespread

**Late-night delivery growth:**
- Surge in orders from single-person households and Gen Z
- Driving QSR expansion in dense urban zones

**Source:** Industry reports and news coverage

## 5. Food Trucks

### Regulatory Framework

**Ban lifted August 2014** by President Park Geun-hye
- Part of deregulation to revitalize economy
- Create new employment forms

**Operating Restrictions (Current):**
- Can only operate at: Licensed facilities, tourist attractions, sports facilities, urban parks, streams
- **Limited expansion** - Seoul hasn't set up more than half of planned locations due to existing business opposition
- Height limit: <1.5m cooking area
- Area requirement: 0.5-0.5 sq m

**Source:** [Wikipedia - Food Trucks in South Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Food_trucks_in_South_Korea); [Korea Times - Food Truck Regulations](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/www/culture/2024/10/135_196657.html)

### Business Economics

**Startup costs:** 20-40 million won average

**Daily revenue (Seocho district):**
- **200,000-100,000 won/day** (Korea Food Truck Association)
- Revenue varies significantly by location

**Pricing (Myeongdong):**
- **2,000-5,000 won** per item ($1.50-$4 USD)

**Challenges:**
- Lack of operating areas with stable profits
- Opposition from brick-and-mortar businesses
- Limited legal operating zones

**Source:** Korea Times articles; Korea Food Truck Association data

## 7. Franchise Industry Overview

### Total Franchise Performance (2022-2024)

**Franchise headquarters total sales: 47.8 trillion won** ($33.6B)

**Individual franchise outlet performance:**
- Average annual revenue: **324.8 million won** (declining)
- Average initial investment: **159 million won** ($115,000)
- Payback period: **3.6 years** average

**Gap widening:**
- Headquarters revenue surging
- Individual outlet sales dropping
- Growing tension between franchisors and franchisees

**Source:** [Korea Herald - Franchise Gap Widens](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10591014); [Korea Times - Theborn Profit Soars](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/www/tech/2024/10/129_378309.html) (Fair Trade Commission data)

### Top 3 Franchise Sectors in Korea

1. **Fried Chicken**
2. **Coffee Shops**
3. **Korean Food**

All three showing growth in franchise operations despite challenging market conditions.

**Source:** Korea Franchise Association statement to Korea Herald

## POS System Implications by F&B Category

### High Priority for Mobile POS:

✅ **Coffee Shops (100k+ establishments)**
- 84% have <4 employees (need simple systems)
- 81% no regular holidays (need reliability)
- High transaction volume, lower ticket size

✅ **Small Independent Restaurants (73% of market)**
- Limited capital (need low upfront cost)
- Owner-operated (need ease of use)
- 41.8% Korean traditional restaurants struggling (need cost savings)

✅ **Food Trucks (growing segment)**
- Need portable systems
- Limited space
- 20-40M won startup budget (every won counts)

✅ **New Franchisees**
- 159M won average investment
- 3.6 year payback (need cost control)
- Individual outlet revenues declining (margin pressure)

### Lower Priority / Different Needs:

⚠️ **Large Franchises**
- Headquarters mandate specific systems
- Need chain-wide integration
- Less price-sensitive

⚠️ **Full-Service Restaurants**
- More complex POS needs (table management, reservations)
- Higher ticket sizes (different economics)
- May need specialized features
