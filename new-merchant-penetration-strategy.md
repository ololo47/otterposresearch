## The Steve Jobs Penetration Strategy

### 1. **Make It Stupid Simple** *(Jobs: "Simple is the ultimate sophistication")*

**The 3-Tap Setup:**
1. Download app from Google Play
2. Connect Bluetooth reader (arrives pre-paired)
3. Tap "Start Selling"

**That's it.** No installers. No training. No manuals.

[Toast claims "single shift setup"](https://pos.toasttab.com/restaurant-pos/new-restaurant-opening) - we do it in **3 minutes**.

### 2. **Bundle the Whole Widget** *(Jobs: "Control the full experience")*

**"Otter Cafe Starter Kit" - ₩299,000** ($225)
- Pre-configured Samsung Galaxy Tab A8
- Bluetooth card reader (paired & tested)
- Thermal receipt printer
- Otter POS app (pre-installed)
- 3 months Premium free
- Korean setup guide (1-page, pictures only)

**Delivered to your door. Ready to sell.**

Compare: [Traditional POS ₩600k-1.5M + weeks of setup](https://tossplace.com/story/recommendation_pos)

**Traditional POS pitch:** "Our system has 47 features including advanced inventory management, employee scheduling, multi-location synchronization..."

**Otter pitch:**

> *"It's your first day. You're terrified. You have 47 things to worry about.*
> *Your POS shouldn't be one of them.*
> *It just works. From day one. No contract. No risk."*

**Emotional, not rational.** Fear drives decisions more than features.

### 4. **Intercept at the Moment of Truth** *(Jobs: "Skate to where the puck is going")*

### 5. **The Toast Growth Playbook (But Better)**

[Toast wins with direct sales teams](https://canvasbusinessmodel.com/blogs/growth-strategy/toast-growth-strategy) - 75% from inbound in mature markets, close to **50% market share in single-location table service**.

**Otter's advantage:** We're lighter, faster, cheaper.

**Their Model:**
- Direct sales reps (expensive)
- "Business in a box" approach
- Strong local presence
- Vertical-specific marketing

**Our Model:**
- Self-service onboarding (cheaper)
- "Business in a box" **hardware bundle** (ours is literal)
- Dealer partnerships (leverage others' presence)
- Hyper-focus on 1 vertical: **new cafes** (not all restaurants)

### 6. **The "First 100" Assault Plan**

**Month 1-2: Perfect the Widget**
- Source 100 Samsung Tab A8 tablets (₩25M investment)
- Source 100 certified Bluetooth readers
- Pre-install, pre-configure, pre-test each unit
- Build Korean support team (3 people)
- Get [NTS e-Tax certification](https://www.cleartax.com/kr/e-invoicing-south-korea)

**Month 3: Stealth Launch**
- 20 beta cafes (free hardware + software)
- Only in Gangnam/Hongdae (high density)
- Obsess over their success
- Film testimonials

**Month 4: Public Launch - "100 Cafe Challenge"**
- "First 100 cafes get Starter Kit for ₩99,000" (₩200k discount)
- No subscription for 6 months
- Guerrilla marketing: Hand-deliver to new cafes opening that week
- Instagram/YouTube blitz with beta testimonials

**Month 5-6: Dealer Blitz**
- Recruit cafe equipment suppliers as dealers
- 30% commission on hardware
- Target: 10 dealers signed
- Let them scale while we perfect product
