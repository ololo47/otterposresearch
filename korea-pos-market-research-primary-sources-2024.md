## 1. Korean Restaurant Industry Crisis (2024)

### Record-Breaking Closures

**107,000+ restaurants closed in 2024** - the highest number on record
- **10.4% closure rate** for full-service restaurants (highest since 2005)
- **Seoul: 13% closure rate**
- **Sejong City: 14.6% closure rate**

**Source:** [Korea Herald - Restaurant closures hit 19-year high](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10419650) (February 2025)

### Historic Net Decline

**First net decline since 2008:** 5,374 more restaurants closed than opened
- From 2019-2023: 100,000+ new restaurants opened annually, offsetting closures
- 2024: The reversal year

**Source:** [Korea Herald](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10419650)

### Economic Pressures

**Statistics Korea (통계청) Data:**
- Delivery-based restaurant sales: **-1.54% year-over-year**
- Restaurant industry output (inflation-adjusted): **-1.9% in 2024** (following -0.6% in 2023)
- Agricultural prices: **+10.4%** (vs 2.3% overall inflation)

**Source:** Korea Herald citing Statistics Korea Nowcast index

### Traditional Korean Restaurants

**504,657 traditional Korean food (Hansik) businesses** operating as of December 2024
- **-1.6% decline** from 512,979 in 2023
- Ministry of Agriculture data

**Source:** [NewKerala - Korean Food Business Shrinks](https://www.newkerala.com/news/o/number-firms-korean-food-business-drops-2024-data-220)

### Startup Statistics (Government Data)

**2023 total startup companies: 1,238,617** (-6.0% from previous year)
- Accommodation/food services: **+8.1% growth** (bright spot)
- Personal services: **+10.0% growth**
- Real estate: **-38.4% decline**

**Source:** [Ministry of SMEs and Startups (중소벤처기업부)](https://www.mss.go.kr/) - Official statistics portal

### Startup Funding

**Only 30% of survey respondents** accessed business loans
- Most relied on: Personal savings, personal loans, credit cards
- **Implication:** Cash-strapped startups need low-cost POS solutions

**Source:** Ministry of SMEs and Startups survey data

## 4. KakaoPay, Naver Pay, Samsung Pay Market Share

### Market Leadership (2023-2024 Data)

**Naver Pay: Leading provider** (per multiple sources)
- **48.9% of mobile payment volume** held by operators like Naver Pay, KakaoPay (2023)
- Slight edge over KakaoPay in usage rates

**KakaoPay: 23+ million active users**
- Typically #2 behind Naver Pay
- **2023 operating income: ₩615 billion won** (up from ₩521B in 2022)

**Source:** [Bank of Korea 2023 Report](https://www.bok.or.kr/); [Statista - KakaoPay Operating Income](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1255140/kakao-pay-revenue/)

### Samsung Pay & Apple Pay

**Samsung Pay:**
- **Most-used financial services app in June 2024: 15.27 million users**
- Works with existing IC card terminals (no new hardware needed)
- **Zero commission fees** for merchants (unlike Apple Pay)

**Apple Pay:**
- Launched March 2023 in Korea
- **Only 70,000 of 2.9 million stores** have compatible NFC readers (~2.4%)
- **0.15% commission fee** vs 0.03% in China, 0.05% in Israel
- **No transit payment support** in Korea (major limitation)

**iPhone adoption rising among youth:**
- **65% of 18-29 year olds** use iPhones (+12% vs prior year)
- Overall: 70% of adults use Samsung phones

**Source:** [Korea Herald - Apple Pay Struggling](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/3372959) (April 2024); [KOMOJU Digital Wallets](https://en.komoju.com/blog/payment-method/korea-digital-wallets/)

### Mobile Phone Manufacturers

**Market share of mobile payment amounts (2023):**
- Electronic operators (KakaoPay, Naver Pay): **48.9%**
- Mobile phone makers (Apple, Samsung): **25.6%** (up from 22.7% in 2021)

**Source:** Bank of Korea 2023 Report

## 5. Delivery Platform Market (2024-2025)

### Market Share Evolution

**November 2024:**
- **Baemin: 60%** (down from previous highs)
- **Coupang Eats + Yogiyo: 40%** combined

**August 2024 breakdown:**
- Baemin: **58.7%**
- Coupang Eats: **22.7%** (doubled since April free delivery launch)
- Yogiyo: **15.1%**

**Early 2025 shifts:**
- Baemin: **57.8%** (down from 71.5% prior year)
- Yogiyo: **5.2%** (down from 9.5%)
- Coupang Eats gaining rapidly

**Source:** [Business Korea - Baemin Struggles](https://www.businesskorea.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=224299); [KED Global - Transaction Drops](https://www.kedglobal.com/retail/newsView/ked202503240003)

### Market Size

**South Korea food delivery market: 20+ trillion won** ($14.4+ billion)
- One of world's largest delivery markets

**Source:** [Korea Herald - Delivery Platform Commissions](http://www.koreaherald.com/view.php?ud=20241114050778)

### Commission Fee Revolution (2025)

**Old rates (through Feb 2025):**
- Baemin: **9.8%**
- Coupang Eats: **9.8%**
- Yogiyo: **9.7%**

**New tiered system (starts Feb 26, 2025):**
Agreement reached November 2024 between government, platforms, restaurant association:
- **Top 35% revenue merchants: 7.8%**
- **35-80% bracket: 6.8%**
- **Bottom 20%: 2%**

**Source:** [Korea Times - Commission Agreement](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/www/nation/2024/11/281_386392.html); [Korea Herald - Feb 26 Implementation](http://www.koreaherald.com/view.php?ud=20241114050778)

## 9. Korea E-Tax Invoice Requirements (2024)

### Threshold Lowered (July 2024)

**New mandate:** Businesses with **annual revenue ≥80 million KRW** must issue e-invoices
- Previous threshold: 100 million KRW
- **Expanding coverage to more small businesses**

**Source:** [Sovos - South Korea E-Tax Invoice](https://sovos.com/vat/tax-rules/electronic-tax-invoices-south-korea/); [ClearTax - Korea E-Invoice 2024](https://www.cleartax.com/kr/e-invoicing-south-korea)

### Core Requirements

**Transmission deadline:**
- **Within 1 day** of invoice issuance to NTS portal
- Must also email to recipient
- Alternative: Monthly summary by 10th of following month

**Format:**
- **XML format** per NTS technical specs
- **PKI certificate digital signature** required
- Domestic transactions only (imports/exports excluded)

**Penalties:**
- **0.3-1% of supply price** for non-compliance

**Archiving:**
- **5 years minimum** retention

**Source:** [SNI Technology - Korea E-Tax System](https://snitechnology.net/south-korea-e-tax-invoice-system/); [Avalara - Korea E-Invoice Requirements](https://www.avalara.com/vatlive/en/country-guides/asia/south-korea/south-korea-e-invoices.html)

### VAT Reporting

**10% VAT collected and remitted quarterly**
- Filing deadline: **25 days after quarter end**
- E-invoices must sync with VAT returns

**Source:** NTS official requirements; [EDICOM - Korea E-Invoicing](https://edicomgroup.com/electronic-invoicing/south-korea)
