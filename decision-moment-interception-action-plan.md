## What We Learned: How Korean POS Companies Actually Do It

### 🎯 **1. The Dealer/Partner Network Model** (Most Common)

#### **Toss Place Example**
**Source:** [Toss Place Partner Strategy](https://blog.toss.im/article/tossplace-partners)

**How They Do It:**
- **Partner Portal:** Dedicated B2B website at [tossplace.com/agency](https://tossplace.com/agency) for dealer recruitment
- **15% market share** in new merchant acquisitions through this channel
- **Financial Incentives:**
  - No upfront payment - dealers pay terminal cost up to 2 months later
  - Up to 90% financing support after installation
  - Revenue share programs for dealers
- **Lead Generation:** Customer service center connects installation requests directly to dealers
- **Dealer Support:** Test terminals, dedicated dealer customer service (separate from merchant support)

**Key Insight:** They eliminated upfront capital requirements (선입금 폐지) to recruit more dealers faster.

**Source:** [Toss Place Opens Dealer-Exclusive Homepage](https://www.thevaluenews.co.kr/news/184320)

---

#### **OK POS Example**
**Source:** [OK POS Official Site](https://www.okpos.co.kr/)

**How They Do It:**
- **260,000 merchants** through dealer network
- **Dealer Management System (ASP):** Web-based portal where dealers manage their merchant locations, licenses, and resources
- **Direct Consultation:** "본사 직접 상담" (headquarters direct consultation) for restaurant/cafe startups
- **Dealer-Specific KakaoTalk Channel:** [OKPOS CEO Channel](https://pf.kakao.com/_jwNJG) for dealer communications

**Key Insight:** Built infrastructure to scale through dealers, not direct sales.

**Source:** [OK POS Dealer Information System](https://okpos.gitbook.io/okpos-guide/0.-okpos/asp)

### 🤝 **2. Delivery Platform Partnerships** (Strategic Integration)

#### **Baemin → Foodtech Investment**
**Source:** [Posfeed Blog - Delivery POS Guide](https://www.posfeed.co.kr/blog/about-delivery-pos-program)

**What Happened:**
- Woowa Brothers (Baemin operator) invested **₩120 billion won** in Foodtech (POS startup) in July 2017
- Foodtech "moved up to leading ranks of POS industry with Baemin's support"
- Integration enables automatic order flow from Baemin → POS system

**Key Insight:** Delivery platforms actively invest in/partner with POS companies to ensure seamless merchant experience.

**Source:** [Posfeed Delivery Integration Systems](https://www.posfeed.co.kr/blog/new-business-delivery-service-apps)

---

#### **Yogiyo Partner Content**
**Source:** [Yogiyo Partner Portal - POS Guide](https://partner.yogiyo.co.kr/content/view/포스-렌탈-vs-구매)

**How They Do It:**
- **Educational Content:** Yogiyo publishes guides for merchants on selecting POS systems
- **Rental vs Purchase Comparison:** Helps new merchants understand options
- **Implicit Recommendations:** By educating merchants, they position themselves as trusted advisors

**Key Insight:** Delivery platforms act as "decision influencers" at the setup phase.

---

#### **Delivery Integration Middleware**
**Source:** [Payhere Delivery Integration](https://help-center.payhere.in/hardware/pc/delivery)

**Major Players:**
- **Posfeed (포스피드):** Integrates 90+ delivery agencies and major platforms
- **Otter Korea (오터코리아):** Delivery aggregation platform
- **Store Angel (매장천사):** Order management system

**Integration Support:**
- Baemin, Yogiyo, Coupang Eats, Naver delivery
- Automatic order transfer from delivery apps to POS
- Multiple delivery agencies (대행) coordination

**Key Insight:** Being pre-integrated with delivery platforms is table stakes. Merchants expect it to "just work."

**Source:** [K-Biz Market Delivery POS Product](https://kbizmarket.com/product/푸드테크-배달포스-배달의민족-요기요-네이버-쿠팡이츠-등-연동/238/)

### 🏪 **3. Equipment Supplier Bundles** (Physical Touchpoint)

#### **DH Mall (DH Coffee Mall)**
**Source:** [dhcoffeemall.com](https://www.dhcoffeemall.com/)

**How They Bundle:**
- **7,000+ cafe installations nationwide**
- **Complete Startup Package:** Coffee machine + grinder + ice maker + ovens + **everything needed**
- **12-month interest-free installment plans**
- **3D design consulting** included
- **After-sales service** network

**Why This Matters:**
New cafe owners buy ALL equipment from one supplier. If that supplier bundles POS, they get it by default.

**Key Insight:** Equipment suppliers are the #1 touchpoint for new cafes. Partner with them.

---

#### **Avillage (Rental Model)**
**Source:** [avillage.co.kr](http://avillage.co.kr/category/패키지렌탈상품/121/)

**Package Contents:**
- Coffee machines (FAEMA, LaCimbali, Nouva Simonelli)
- Water heaters, grinders
- 36-month rental option
- "Cafe Startup Package Rental" positioning

**Key Insight:** Rental packages lower barrier for cash-strapped startups. POS should be part of this.

---

#### **Machine Market**
**Source:** [machinemarket.kr](https://m.machinemarket.kr/category/카페장비/129)

**Offerings:**
- New & used equipment
- "Startup Package" (창업 패키지) category
- Direct trade for lowest prices

**Key Insight:** New owners are price shopping. Being listed on equipment marketplaces = discoverability.

### 🏢 **4. Business Registration Services** (Weak Opportunity)

**Source:** [K-Startup Portal](https://www.k-startup.go.kr/)

**What Exists:**
- Government portal for startup support programs
- Business registration assistance from consultants ([Pearson Korea](https://pearsonkorea.com/insights/Korea-Company-Setup-2024-StepbyStep-Registration-Guide/), [KOISRA UP](https://www.koisraup.com/business-registration-korea/))
- Tax office registration within 20 days ([NTS](https://www.nts.go.kr/nts/cm/cntnts/cntntsView.do?mi=2444&cntntsId=7777))

**Gap Found:**
❌ **No evidence of POS bundling in business registration packages**
❌ **No tax office partnerships for POS at registration**

**Key Insight:** This is an **untapped opportunity**. No Korean POS company is intercepting at government registration touchpoints yet.
